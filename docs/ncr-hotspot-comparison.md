## What remote research added

**BGC parking destinations.** The estate's [official FAQ](https://bgc.com.ph/faqs/)
names High Street Central and South parking, Park Triangle open/basement parking,
open-air parking along 7th Avenue and nearby office/retail basements. This adds
specific first-party investigation leads to the existing parking-inquiry channel.
It does not confirm the visitor conditions, entrance, rates or present operation
of any mapped feature. An estate's safety statement is an operator claim, not an
independent safety finding.

**Cubao operational context.** The [current Araneta guide](https://aranetacity.com/parking)
still names eight parking locations, entrances P1–P19, and busy weekends,
paydays and Coliseum event nights. These are the operator's published claims.
Transient slot figures were deliberately excluded, and the guide supplies no
validated current rate card. Mall opening hours cannot be copied as parking
facility hours. Parking Garage South's exact name also appears in the licensed
OSM snapshot (`osm:way/184362727:MultiPolygon`), providing a useful identity
review lead; its usable access and exact vehicle entrance still need a visit.

**Makati has historical measurements, not a current demand score.** The
university-hosted [2013 TSSP conference paper](https://ncts.upd.edu.ph/tssp/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Valdez13.pdf),
_Comparison of On-Street Parking Management in Ermita-Malate Manila and Makati
Central Business District_, reports MaPA data for **one Friday, 19 October 2012,
07:00–17:00**. It reports an afternoon peak interval at **14:30** and average
Makati parking duration of **1.164 hours per vehicle** (PDF pages 8, 11–12).
These are the authors' historical results; original MaPA records were not
obtained or reproduced. They justify a replication question in Legazpi/Salcedo
and nearby CBD areas, not a 2026 peak forecast, occupancy estimate or market-size
calculation. The paper's separate Ermita-Malate payment/WTP questionnaires do not
establish current Makati, Poblacion or Parkey willingness to pay.

**National context is not hotspot demand.** A [JICA transport policy manual](https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12247664.pdf)
discusses parking charges as a transport-demand-management option using
historical data and scenarios. Scenario revenues, car-mode shares and trip totals
were not imported as current observed demand or Parkey revenue. Destination
popularity and business-district labels remain trip-purpose proxies.

## Tomas Morato: the full ordinance changes the research question

The [official SP-3345 S-2024 PDF](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/SP-3345-S-2024.pdf)
is an eight-page scanned document. Local Windows OCR extracted its text; the
research register records that method and its fallibility. The following is a
source summary, **not legal approval or a current implementation determination**:

- Sections 2 and 4 cover Tomas Morato **from Scout Rallos to Don A. Roces Avenue**.
  Motorized traversal and parking are prohibited **06:00–10:00 on Sundays,
  subject to Section 5's implementation phases**.
- Section 5's first phase applies on the **first Sunday of each month for the
  first year after IRR approval**. The next phase is **every other Sunday**, with
  TWG monitoring/recommendations before full implementation.
- Section 6 identifies exemptions including emergency/police vehicles and
  establishment delivery vehicles. Section 9 requires signage/advisories.

The IRR approval date, amendments, current phase and enforcement were not found
in the retrieved evidence. **Do not encode a blanket all-Sunday closure or use
the ordinance title to establish parking rights.** The human check is the exact
block, posted schedule, current implementing notice and authority response.
Observe access to off-street alternatives without driving through a restricted
section. This is a legal-risk learning priority, not proof of unmet parking
demand.

National [RA 4136 sections 46, 52 and 54](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1964/ra_4136_1964.html)
also describe prohibited parking locations, sidewalk restrictions and obstruction.
The Official Gazette request returned 403; the accessible reference is LawPhil's
university-hosted text. Neither this reference nor missing signs in one photograph
establishes site-specific permission. Relevant current local rules still require
accountable review.

## Operator, host and identity opportunities

- **Cubao and BGC:** named portfolios and published inquiry paths make a future
  authorized operator discussion concrete. They provide neither an agreement
  nor evidence that inventory is available to Parkey. Existing estate discovery
  tools are also competition/context to investigate.
- **Poblacion, Makati and Ortigas:** existing named lots and licensed operator
  tags provide a field sample. The comparison JSON preserves source IDs and
  edit dates for corporate/public-operator hints. A tag is not confirmation that
  the named entity currently manages a facility or can offer spaces.
- **North EDSA:** an apartment/condominium parking-host hypothesis requires exact
  property identity, space-holder authority, condominium/customer rules and
  an authorized response. The attempted SMDC Grass paths returned 404. A Pasay
  corporate footer address was deliberately not reused as the Grass location.
  No competing Grass coordinate was silently selected.
- **Chemin Neuf conflict:** the [official local contact page](https://www.chemin-neuf.ph/where-to-find-us/contact-us/)
  gives **2486 Legarda Street, Sampaloc, Barangay 412** and the local site describes
  a Christian community/student center. That is useful non-parking identity
  context, not proof that incidental parking is absent or publicly usable. The
  exact source-point/entrance/category conflict remains for review. No contact
  details were imported and no message was sent.
- **Additional areas considered:** the historical Ermita-Malate study is a
  comparison-method lead. A [Pasig municipal advisory](https://pasigcity.gov.ph/news-and-releases/abiso-pasig-city-mega-parking-i-near-mutya-ng-pasig-mega-market-caruncho-ave-brgy-san-nicolas-584)
  concerns only Mega Parking I's third floor on **7 October 2026, 08:00–12:00**,
  with other floors open and reopening planned for 12:01. It does **not** establish
  a current whole-facility closure or justify promoting a new launch hotspot.

The official QC SP-3234 S-2023 notice concerns an initial-rate exemption for
QC-resident PWDs in **city-government-owned** parking facilities. Its full
conditions/current implementation were not reviewed; it cannot be generalized
to private mall parking or converted to an unconditional “Free” label.

## Priority method and human handoff

The first wave is an **ordered research-logistics choice**:

1. **Poblacion:** close the six preserved owner tasks and their known questions
   before adding competing fieldwork; retain every original navigation reference.
2. **Cubao:** use named facilities and a single estate's published operational
   claim to design a repeatable ordinary-day/event comparison.
3. **BGC:** verify the first-party parking leads and visitor conditions, then
   seek an authorized response through the published channel when approved.
4. **Tomas Morato:** conduct a separately planned rule/sign/access check, with
   the implemented phase established before a Sunday vehicle route.

The second wave compares Binondo, Makati CBD, Ortigas and MOA. North EDSA,
Alabang and Maginhawa remain geographic/operating-model comparison strata.
Include an afternoon Makati sample as a historical replication question if
safe and practical, rather than treating 14:30 as a current expected peak.
Use the candidate-specific fieldwork pack and source-coordinate navigation
links generated by the data-review workstream; this report does not replace
exact entrance, consent or access instructions.

No numeric opportunity metric has been assigned. Every area lacks comparable
current evidence for all mandatory dimensions: demand, usable supply gap,
willingness to pay, partner potential and launch feasibility. An accountable
human must choose the final hotspot and authorize any publication. Physical
observations, authorized operator/owner participation, consenting driver research
and legal interpretation remain the genuine human work.

## Retrieval limits and reproducibility

General Bing responses returned unrelated results despite HTTP 200; their
snippets were rejected as evidence. DuckDuckGo required a human challenge, Yahoo
returned 500, and several operator domains failed TLS or returned generic shells.
Those restrictions were not bypassed. Direct operator/government/university
pages yielded the scoped facts above; failed rate searches were not filled by
guessed tariffs, copied social-media imagery or restricted Maps data.

Raw responses and OCR output remain in ignored `output/ncr/hotspot-extended*`.

hints are committed. No general license to republish website content is assumed.
