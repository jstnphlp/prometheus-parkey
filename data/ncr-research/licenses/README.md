The normalized database derives from OpenStreetMap. To the extent Parkey holds database rights in that derivative, those rights are made available under ODbL 1.0. The source archives and normalized research data distributed here provide the corresponding derivative database; retain its notices and share-alike obligations when redistributing it. Original record content retains its own declared licenses; this does not relicense third-party copyrights.

Overture source access: [public Azure release](https://overturemapswestus2.blob.core.windows.net/release/2026-10-21.0/theme=places/type=place/). CDLA-Permissive-2.0 requires its agreement text to accompany shared data; that text is included. Apache-2.0 records retain their declared provider attribution and are accompanied by the full license. The Azure record declarations are the evidence for these assignments. The official Foursquare NOTICE linked by Overture was subsequently retrieved successfully from https://opensource.foursquare.com/places-notice-txt/. Its complete visible wording is retained in [Foursquare-NOTICE.txt](Foursquare-NOTICE.txt), with HTML whitespace normalized and source hashes in [foursquare-notice-source.json](foursquare-notice-source.json). [PARKEY-MODIFICATIONS.txt](PARKEY-MODIFICATIONS.txt) separately documents research filtering and normalization. Preserve these notices with the full Apache agreement. This resolves the missing-notice retrieval task, without declaring legal publication approval.

Boundary source: [geoBoundaries API](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM3/), [original administrative dataset](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/philippines-administrative-levels-0-to-3), and [CC BY 3.0 IGO terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/). These historical boundaries are research geography, including known EMBO/reclamation caveats, and do not assert current legal city membership. No source or rights holder endorses Parkey.

Modifications comprise geographic filtering, parking category normalization, explicit safety/evidence labels, source identity deduplication and aggregate research statistics. The raw source identities, geometry, attribution and hashes remain traceable. No Google Maps data was used. Information is supplied without warranty under the applicable licenses; licensing does not establish access rights to a site.

License texts were retrieved on 2026-11-05. SPDX's license-list-data mirror supplied the CDLA, ODbL and CC BY IGO texts; Apache supplied its own text. The canonical OpenDataCommons text endpoint returned HTTP 403, so its SPDX mirror was used. [sources.json](sources.json) records URLs and SHA-256 hashes of the included license text files. These are documented source declarations and distribution notices, not a formal legal opinion.
